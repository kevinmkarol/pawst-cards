How rating works
  • A dashed outline is a proposed rating, or a frame left out with ✕. Correct what is wrong, then save.
  • Best and good are worth sending. Okay is the pet, but nothing to send. ✕ leaves a frame out of training.
  • Click a photo to flip it between good and bad.
  • Keys: 0 to 3 rate the frame under the pointer, X leaves it out, Enter saves, S skips.
